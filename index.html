How Karibu works

1. Profile

Photos, age, bio, interests, county, faith and lifestyle. Student, professional or church mode.

2. Nearby

We ask for location (or a town you pick). Matches rank by distance, then shared interests.

3. Match & chat

Like someone. If they like you back, you unlock chat. Report and block are always one tap away.

Meet people where you actually are

Safety first

Report, block, and photo guidelines. Never share M-Pesa PINs or send money to someone you have not met in a public place. This demo stores data only in your browser.
